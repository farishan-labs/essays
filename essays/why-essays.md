# Why essays

Research topics are structured, verified, and dated. They are not meant to be read
end to end. Essays are the narrative layer on top: one idea, one argument, one
takeaway, written to be read.

The research repo answers "what did we find?" The essays repo answers "what do we
think about it?" Keeping them separate keeps both honest: research stays
reproducible, essays stay voice-driven.

Promote a topic to an essay when it has a point of view worth stating, not just
findings worth recording.
